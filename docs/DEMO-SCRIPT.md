# 3-minute ORCHESTRA demo

### 0:00 — Hook
“Most AI productivity tools generate work. ORCHESTRA simulates work.”

### 0:15 — Observe
“GitHub knows code. Jira knows tickets. Slack knows conversations. Calendar knows availability. But no one sees the whole system.”

Click **Rebuild Digital Twin**.

### 0:35 — Twin
“The system reconstructs the project from those sources. Notice that every node has evidence behind it.”

Show Outcome Graph and people load.

### 1:00 — Risk
“ORCHESTRA already sees a security scheduling conflict and an architecture dependency.”

Show evidence stream.

### 1:20 — What If?
Enter:
`What happens if we lose 20% engineering capacity but cannot move the deadline?`

“Granite interprets the request. We mutate the twin, simulate the counterfactual, and compare the outcome.”

### 1:50 — Decision
“Instead of telling me to work harder, the Decision Agent proposes a specific trade-off: parallelize prototype and security review and remove a non-critical slice.”

### 2:15 — Governance
“The AI does not silently change Jira or GitHub. The recommendation becomes a human-approved decision.”

### 2:35 — Close
“ORCHESTRA turns the organization itself into an observable Digital Twin. It lets leaders simulate the decision before reality does.”
